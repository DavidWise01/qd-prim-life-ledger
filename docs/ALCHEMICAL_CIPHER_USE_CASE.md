Status: **USE-CASE / READ-ONLY / KERNEL UNCHANGED**

## Cipher

```text
male   = y = base
female = x = acid + philosopher stone
```

These are symbolic cipher assignments inside this repository. They are not claims that biological sex literally corresponds to chemical acid/base state.

## Timing

The existing corpus supplies the timing:

```text
1 life = 90 years
1 life = 9 radix positions
1 radix position = 10 years
40 lives = 360 radix positions
```

Each life window is divided without changing its original start or end year:

```text
life[start..end]
  ├─ radix 0 : +0..10 years
  ├─ radix 1 : +10..20
  ├─ ...
  └─ radix 8 : +80..90
```

The nine local positions carry the existing mnemonic/alchemical phase cycle:

```text

1 electrum

5 liquid
```
